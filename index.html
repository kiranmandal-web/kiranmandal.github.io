About

I am an Integrated Ph.D. researcher in Theoretical Physics at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), Hyderabad. My research lies at the intersection of condensed matter theory, quantum optics, and hybrid quantum systems, with a focus on understanding non-equilibrium dynamics and emergent collective phenomena.


Before joining TIFR Hyderabad, I completed my B.Sc. in Physics at the University of Calcutta. My current work centers on analytical modeling and computational simulations—investigating cavity magnonics, hybridized magnon-polariton modes, and topological signatures in low-dimensional spin lattices. I explore how strong light-matter interactions can be leveraged to probe and control quantum states of matter, bridging fundamental many-body physics with quantum information architectures.


Research Interests: Condensed Matter Theory, Cavity Magnonics, Quantum Optics, Topological Systems.
